The score of a strategy is calculated as:

`(4 × 1 month performance %) + (3 × 3 month performance %) + (2 × 6 month performance %) + (1 × 1 year performance %)`

The fee-weighted score is:

`score × (1 - management fee %) × (1 - performance fee %) × (1 - entry fee %) × (1 - exit fee %)`

All scores/returns on this page are normalised in the range 0&ndash;100.
